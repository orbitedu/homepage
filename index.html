What We Offer

Our Services

Comprehensive support at every stage of your academic journey.

Graduate School Application

End-to-end support for Master's and PhD applications — school selection, statement of purpose, letters of recommendation, interview preparation, and final decisions.

Summer Workshop Programs

Curated short-term programs at top universities that build academic credentials, expand networks, and give students a meaningful taste of campus life abroad.

Overseas Study Programs

Semester and year-long study abroad placements, including housing, enrollment support, visa guidance, and on-the-ground orientation for a smooth transition.

US–China Joint Programs

Advisory for joint undergraduate degrees offered through partnerships between Chinese and American universities — helping students understand pathways, credits, and requirements.
